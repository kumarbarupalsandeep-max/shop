# Phase 02 — UI/UX and Brand Design System

Status: In progress — neutral design groundwork.
Dependency note: Phase 01 still has business approval gates. Phase 02 proceeds only with reversible, brand-neutral work allowed by the Phase 01 decision pack. This does not mark Phase 01 complete.

## Objective
Produce an original, accessible, responsive and implementation-ready design system for the single-vendor Indian golf commerce platform.

## Confirmed inputs
- Single-vendor golf e-commerce for India.
- Initial confirmed product groups: golf balls, apparel, footwear, bags and accessories.
- Own delivery team direction.
- Next.js + React storefront, NestJS REST API and PostgreSQL planning baseline.
- Customer/admin page inventory documented in Phase 01.

## Open inputs
- Legal seller/customer-facing brand identity, logo, domain and brand approver.
- Final launch assortment and whether clubs/pre-owned/junior categories are included.
- Actual product image rights and content.
- Final shipping, return, payment and support claims.
- Named product/design approver.

## Definition of done
1. All P02 workstreams have deliverables and evidence.
2. Customer/admin page layouts cover approved P0 page inventory.
3. Tokens and component states are consistent and implementable.
4. Responsive layouts pass agreed viewport matrix.
5. Keyboard and screen-reader checks are recorded.
6. WCAG 2.2 AA review has no unresolved critical blockers, or exceptions are approved.
7. Brand owner approves logo, palette, typography, imagery and content.
8. Product owner approves page flows, responsive behavior and design handoff.
9. Handoff includes assets, spacing/type/color tokens, component states and interaction notes.
10. Phase 02 exit sign-off is recorded.

## Current blocker
No legal brand name, logo, official palette, approved photography, named approver or final assortment approval is recorded. Therefore neutral design specification can progress, but Phase 02 cannot be marked formally 100% approved until owner inputs and visual QA evidence exist.
