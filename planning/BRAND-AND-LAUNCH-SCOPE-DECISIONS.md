# Business Decision Pack — Brand and Launch Scope

**Phase:** 01 — Planning and Architecture
**Status:** Decision brief; no option is approved until the business owner confirms it.

## 1. Brand identity
Required before public UI approval:
- Legal business/seller name and registered address.
- Customer-facing brand name and approved spelling.
- Logo files and usage rights.
- Brand colours, typography and tone of voice.
- Domain and official support email/phone.
- Brand asset owner and approval authority.

## 2. Launch assortment decision
Current confirmed product groups from project brief: golf balls, apparel, footwear, bags and accessories.

Open assortment decisions:
| Decision | Option A | Option B | Recommendation for planning only |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product condition | New products only | New + pre-owned | Start new-only unless pre-owned grading, warranty and returns are operationally ready |
| Golf clubs | Exclude initially | Include clubs/sets | Exclude until supplier data and specialist variant rules are verified |
| Apparel segments | Limited initial range | Men, women, junior | Start only with actual supplier stock and size charts |
| Accessories | Core accessories | Broad specialist/electronics range | Start with stocked items and warranty/support clarity |
| Personalization | Not offered | Custom engraving/printing | Defer until proof approval, lead times and returns rules exist |

Recommendations are not approvals. Confirm against the first actual stock list.

## 3. Product readiness evidence
For every launch SKU, collect:
- Supplier/manufacturer and authorized data source.
- Product title, category, model and accurate description.
- Variant options and unique SKU.
- Product images and usage rights.
- Barcode/GTIN if supplied.
- MRP/selling price and finance-approved tax/HSN data.
- Stock quantity and receiving evidence.
- Country of origin/importer information where applicable.
- Warranty, care and return restrictions where applicable.
