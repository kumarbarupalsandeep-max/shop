# Phase 02 — Golf Commerce UI/UX Benchmark Research

Research date: 14 November 2026. Purpose: extract broad UX patterns for an original Indian golf commerce storefront; this is not a reproduction exercise.

## St Andrews Links Shop — https://shop.standrews.com/homepage
- Premium brand storytelling appears alongside product discovery.
- Shipping, secure checkout, returns and customer service reassurance are visible.
- Navigation separates audience collections and curated collections; newsletter and social discovery sit in the lower-page experience.
- Design implication: use an editorial hero and compact trust strip, with original copy and authorized assets only.

## Golf Mart India — https://golfmartindia.com/
- Deep category navigation covers clubs, balls, sets, deals, bags, footwear, accessories, trolleys, juniors, pre-owned and apparel.
- Homepage exposes top categories and best-selling products.
- Product cards show price/discount context and distinguish products with selectable variants from direct add-to-cart products.
- Design implication: make taxonomy and variant selection easy to scan; mobile filters must remain usable.
- Scope caution: broad competitor assortment does not approve every category for this project's P0 launch.

## Golf Garage — https://golfgarage.in/
- Navigation separates new and pre-owned equipment, accessories, sell-related entry, services and content.
- Pre-owned products are presented as a distinct discovery area.
- Services such as coaching, consulting and club fitting have their own navigation.
- Design implication: if pre-owned or services are approved later, give them distinct templates and clear condition/service information.

## Golfoy — https://golfoy.com
- Direct page inspection was blocked with HTTP 403 during this research run.
- No detailed feature claims are made.
- Re-audit manually or from an accessible source before using it as an evidence-backed benchmark.

## Reusable patterns
1. Premium editorial hero with a clear shopping action.
2. Category tiles with strong product photography and simple labels.
3. Product cards with image, title, variant context, price, availability and one clear action.
4. Trust/service reassurance near conversion points.
5. Search, filters, sort and pagination with mobile-friendly controls.
6. Product detail gallery with clear variant selection and delivery/returns information.
7. Cart and checkout exposing final costs before order placement.
8. Separate admin UI patterns from customer-facing storefront styling.

## Do not copy
- Do not copy logos, brand names, text, photography, exact layout or distinctive visual treatment.
- Do not use competitor product data, prices or imagery as the live catalogue.
- Do not imply authorized reseller relationships without evidence.
- Do not assume pre-owned, club fitting, trade-in, coaching, rewards or loyalty are approved P0 features.

## Research limitations
- Visible-page UX audit only; not a full accessibility, performance, checkout, legal or security audit.
- Site content can change; recheck before final design approval.
- Golfoy was inaccessible in this run and requires a separate review.

## Accessibility standard reference
- W3C, Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Recommendation: https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/ — use the normative success criteria for audit; supporting quick-reference material is guidance, not a replacement for the standard.
- WCAG 2.2 includes additional success criteria beyond WCAG 2.1. The project targets WCAG 2.2 AA as a design baseline; conformance is not claimed until evaluated.
