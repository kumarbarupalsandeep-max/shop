# Golf Retail Reference Website Audit

**Project:** Golf Commerce Platform  
**Research date:** 14 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Feature and UX research for planning. These observations are not requirements until approved.

## 1. St Andrews Shop — premium brand and collections
Source: https://shop.standrews.com/homepage

Observed:
- Navigation separates New In, Men, Women, Accessories, Kids, Brands and Gift Guide.
- Collection-led merchandising and themed product stories are prominent.
- Personalisation, gift cards, new arrivals and featured brands are used as discovery paths.
- Trust and service cues include official merchandise, worldwide shipping, secure checkout, returns and customer service.

Ideas to evaluate:
- Premium editorial hero and curated collections.
- Gift guide and seasonal landing pages.
- Personalisation only if operationally supported.
- Clear delivery, returns, secure checkout and customer-service links.

Do not copy its brand assets, photography, text or distinctive visual execution.

## 2. Golfoy — breadth of golf assortment and product merchandising
Sources:
- https://golfoy.com/discount-section.html
- https://golfoy.com/golf-accessories.html
- https://golfoy.com/all-golf-brands-india/titleist-golf-india/titleist-categories.html

Observed:
- Broad navigation across clubs, apparel, balls, footwear, gloves, bags, training, trolleys, junior, women's, grips and accessories.
- Product listings expose MRP, deal price, savings, reviews, wishlists and quantity/offer messaging.
- Golf-specific taxonomy includes club types, bag types, grips by size/type, headcovers, rangefinders, training and on-course accessories.
- Some product dimensions are specialist-specific: handedness, club type, apparel sizing, etc.
- The site promotes loyalty/points, best-price messaging, free shipping and easy returns.

Ideas to evaluate:
- Search/filter taxonomy that understands golf-specific attributes.
- Product detail pages with variant and fit guidance.
- Transparent price/discount breakdown and verified review controls.
- Loyalty only after margin, accounting and redemption rules are approved.

## 3. Golf Mart India — multi-brand catalog and category navigation
Sources:
- https://golfmartindia.com/
- https://golfmartindia.com/shop/

Observed:
- Homepage features top categories, best sellers, new arrivals, clearance/deals and curated collections.
- Catalog filters include price and nested category/brand groupings.
- Product cards show sale price, crossed-out MRP, wishlist and variant selection where relevant.
- Catalog includes clubs, package sets, balls, bags, footwear, apparel and accessories.

Ideas to evaluate:
- Layered category and brand filters.
- Category landing pages and curated product groups.
- Clear variant selection from product cards only where the selected option is unambiguous.

## 4. Golf Garage — pre-owned, trade-in and service operations
Sources:
- https://golfgarage.in/pages/trade-in
- https://golfgarage.in/pages/faq
- https://golfgarage.in/policies/refund-policy

Observed:
- New and pre-owned equipment are offered, with trade-in enquiry and workshop/repair services.
- Pre-owned products require inspection, authenticity checks and visible condition grading.
- The published refund policy distinguishes new from pre-owned equipment, and describes different eligibility/conditions.
- Trade-in valuation is subject to physical inspection and final offer.

Ideas to evaluate:
- Keep pre-owned inventory and condition grading separate from new product workflows.
- Require inspection evidence, condition notes and multiple photos.
- Separate pre-owned returns/warranty rules from new-product rules.
- Trade-in should be a later module unless launch scope and operating controls are approved.

## 5. Cross-site product requirements to consider

| Capability | Business value | Planning decision |
|---|---|---|
| Golf-specific taxonomy | Customers find specialist products | Include in catalog design |
| Brand and price filters | Faster product discovery | Confirm actual inventory |
| Size/colour/hand/shaft variants | Correct purchasable SKU | Define per category |
| Product reviews | Buyer confidence | P1 candidate; moderation needed |
| Wishlist | Save products | P1 candidate |
| Deals/discounts | Merchandising | Only with approved pricing rules |
| Loyalty/points | Repeat purchases | P1 candidate; accounting rules needed |
| Gift cards | Gifting and stored value | Later; legal/accounting review |
| Pre-owned grading | Transparent used-equipment condition | Out of P0 unless explicitly approved |
| Trade-in | Used inventory acquisition | Later; inspection and valuation process required |
| Repair/workshop | Service revenue | Out of P0 unless business confirms |

## 6. Recommended direction for this project

Recommended P0 planning baseline (requires owner approval):
1. Launch with new products only.
2. Initial categories: balls, apparel, footwear, bags and accessories.
3. Add golf clubs/package sets only after confirming inventory and product expertise.
4. Use category-specific attributes; do not force all products into generic size/colour fields.
5. Keep pre-owned, trade-in, repair, loyalty, gift cards and reviews out of P0 until policies and operating owners are defined.
6. Use reference websites for feature ideas only; create original brand, layout, copy and visual system.

## 7. Research limitations
- This is a public-page snapshot; pages, stock, prices, policies and features may change.
- No competitor revenue, conversion, traffic or customer satisfaction claims are made.
- Competitor policies must not be copied as this project's policy.
- Every launch policy must be approved for the actual business and reviewed by relevant legal/tax advisors.


## 8. Additional Golfoy detail verified in a second source pass
Sources:
- https://golfoy.com/about-golfoy
- https://golfoy.com/
- https://golfoy.com/golf-accessories.html
- https://golfoy.com/bags.html
- https://golfoy.com/custom-golf-gears

Additional observations:
- Golfoy describes a customer rewards program where account holders earn redeemable Golf Bucks; its current site footer also links to gift cards, trade-in, bulk orders and dealer enquiries.
- The catalog includes specialist golf equipment such as grips, rangefinders, trolleys, simulator screens, performance wearables, and golf gifts, beyond the five initial categories in this project's confirmed brief.
- Product listing pages expose rating/review counts, MRP, deal price, savings, quantity and wishlist controls.
- Bag filters include brand, construction, feel, colour, divider count, pockets, price and express-shipping availability.
- Custom product pages show personalization options for balls, apparel, headwear, umbrellas, bags and accessories; this implies a customization workflow and proof/approval requirements if ever adopted.
- The About page makes claims about company history, customers and product counts; these are competitor self-reported statements and are not independently validated here.

Project implication: do not copy the competitor's product range or rewards promise. Use these observations to create an approved category-specific filter matrix. Treat customization, loyalty, gift cards, trade-in, bulk orders and dealer workflows as separate optional modules, not automatic P0 scope.
